DICOM RT — Feature Tour · Version 0.2.2

Open index.html in a modern browser. Keep screens and media together. The presentation works offline, without external fonts or network requests.

Arrow keys: slides. F: fullscreen. Click images or use Enlarge for images and videos. The opening slide displays five actual viewer clips immediately; select a smaller preview to bring it forward. The requested opening videos autoplay immediately, including with reduced-motion enabled. Pause previews stops video playback. Reduced-motion disables automatic tile cycling and CSS transitions. Seven MP4 clips can also be played separately.

All pictures and footage show the actual WPF viewer. The main case is the authorized nonpatient public benchmark. The dual-layer MLC clip is an approved sanitized MLC-only capture with no patient image or identifiers. There are no synthetic presentation images. Fusion and multi-plan summation are described as capabilities; the benchmark does not depict them.

HyperFrames compositions show rendered states, not screen-recorded responsiveness or treatment delivery time. DVH, contour reformatting and 3D meshes are approximate previews, not clinical validation.
