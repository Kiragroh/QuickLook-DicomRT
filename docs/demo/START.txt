DICOM RT — Feature Tour

Open index.html in a modern browser. Keep the screens and media folders together. The presentation works offline, without external fonts or network requests.

Arrow keys: slides. F: fullscreen. Click images to enlarge. Four MP4 clips can also be played separately.

Views were rendered by the actual WPF component. HyperFrames compositions animate actual rendered states; these are demonstrations, not screen-recorded responsiveness measurements. The main case is the user-authorised nonpatient benchmark M01. Registered CT/MR fusion and the two-plan sum use clearly labelled synthetic fixtures.
