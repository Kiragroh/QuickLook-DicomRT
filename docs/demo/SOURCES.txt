QuickLook DICOM RT 0.2.6 feature tour

Clinical-looking imagery: actual ViewerControl captures from the explicitly approved public, nonpatient multi-metastasis benchmark. 191 CT files, one RTSTRUCT, one RTPLAN and one RTDOSE. The captured selection contains 24 PTVs and Brainstem (source type AVOIDANCE). No private case imagery or synthetic anatomy appears.

Conceptual artwork: built-in GPT Image generated the Space key and direct-file graphics. Exact prompts and provenance: art/imagegen-prompts.md.

Film: deterministic local HyperFrames 0.8.78 + GSAP composition at 1600 x 900, 30 fps, 106 seconds. Actual settled UI capture sequences are nominally 15 fps. The film is edited to explain features; it is not a real-time interaction benchmark, treatment delivery timing or clinical validation.

Authoring reference: https://github.com/nateherkai/hyperframes-student-kit

Capabilities outside this dataset: MR/spatial registration, dual-layer MLC and multiple-plan dose summation. These are described, not depicted as if present in the benchmark.

Capture notes: CAPTURE-MANIFEST.json. Viewer source and tests: https://github.com/Kiragroh/QuickLook-DicomRT
